Hi, I'm **Lazy eNGineer**.

I'm a field engineer in manufacturing, based in Korea. I'm too lazy to do the same thing twice, so I automate repetitive work with AI, one task at a time.

Every week I write up one automation that actually saved me time — written so that non-developers can follow along.

- Each post follows the same flow: the annoying task → how I automated it → time saved → how you can do it too.
- All sample data and screenshots are made from scratch for illustration.

"Lazy eNGineer" is a nod to the idea that good engineers are lazy in the right way: let machines handle the repetitive work, and spend your time on what matters.
